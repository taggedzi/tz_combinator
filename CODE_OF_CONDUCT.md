# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

`tz_combinator` aims to be a welcoming, accessible, and constructive project.
Everyone participating in the repository is expected to help create an
environment where people can contribute without harassment, intimidation, or
needless hostility.

People have different communication styles, abilities, schedules, levels of
experience, and access to time and technology. Good-faith questions,
clarification requests, and mistakes are welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and changes rather than people.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to correction.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, and requests to stop an interaction.
- Use plain language where practical and explain uncommon terminology.
- Give credit for ideas and contributions.
- Keep discussions relevant and move extended disagreements toward a clear
  technical question or decision.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Personal attacks, insults, ridicule, or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption, bad-faith argument, spam, or manipulation.
- Pressuring anyone to disclose a disability, health condition, identity, or
  personal circumstances.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Maintainer capacity and boundaries

This is a small, unpaid, single-maintainer project. Participation does not
create an entitlement to immediate replies, implementation, review, support,
or acceptance of a contribution. Delayed responses usually reflect limited
capacity, disability, health, or other responsibilities—not the value of the
person or report.

The maintainer may pause a discussion, limit repetitive contact, close an
inactive item, or decline work that the project cannot sustainably support.
These boundaries must be applied consistently and respectfully.

Contributors may also step away at any time. No explanation is required.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, code review, repository
discussions, and other public or private communication undertaken as part of
this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an ordinary misunderstanding, a calm request to change course is often
enough, but direct engagement is never required.

For a sensitive conduct concern:

1. Do not post private or identifying details in a public issue.
2. Use a private contact method listed on the
   [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/taggedzi), if one is
   available.
3. For threats, abuse, privacy violations, or platform-wide misconduct, use
   [GitHub's reporting and blocking tools](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Include only the information needed to understand the concern, such as links,
dates, and a brief description. Do not collect or send unrelated personal
information.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, ask participants to stop, restrict
participation, temporarily lock a discussion, or ban a participant. Responses
will consider context, impact, patterns of behavior, safety, and the needs of
affected people. Confidentiality will be respected as far as reasonably
possible, but complete confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when action or
legal compliance requires disclosure.

If a report concerns the sole maintainer, GitHub's reporting tools are the
independent escalation path.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) and adapted for
a small, single-maintainer project.
